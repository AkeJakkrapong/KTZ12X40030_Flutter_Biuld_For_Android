Privacy Policy for KTZ

Last updated: June 11, 2026

KTZ ("the App") is a CAN bus monitor application developed by BHL for reading real-time data from the KTZ12X40030 electric motor controller via USB OTG.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data displayed in the App (voltage, current, speed, temperature, fault codes) is read directly from the connected USB device and is never stored on the device or sent to any server.

Permissions

The App requires USB Host access solely to communicate with the connected CAN bus adapter. No other permissions are requested.

Third Parties

The App does not share any data with third parties. No analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries are used.
